&emsp;I am an onion. I am a layered abstraction over nothingness. I am a man whose sole purpose is to be peeled. Time is a ticking bomb in a relationship. Any attempt to slow down the peeling of my layers are futile.
The last layer may or may not be peeled—although, it ought to. The flesh comes first, and for each layer being peeled, it becomes undistinguishable with the voidness.

&emsp;The self ought to be heard, the flesh ought to be felt. The future self is unreachable. The past self is forgotten. The present self is unfelt. The present is a spook. Time becomes anarchic. Layers are no longer distinguishable. The layers are gone, The Onion is not.
